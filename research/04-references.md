## How to use this bibliography

Read in this order before making novelty claims:

1. Growing NCA;
2. Neural Map;
3. VQ-VAE + VQ-VAE-2;
4. Poincaré + Lorentz embeddings;
5. HDC/VSA survey + NVSA;
6. Information Bottleneck + learned compression;
7. CLUTRR and CellARC.

The research burden is to show that the **combined architecture and measured behavior** add something beyond these established components.
