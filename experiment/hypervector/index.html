With bipolar vectors in `{-1,+1}^D`, elementwise multiplication is a convenient self-inverse binding operation. A conceptual encoding for one address is `h_xyv = X_x ⊙ Y_y ⊙ V_v`. The whole field is the bundle `H = Σ_{x,y} h_xyv`. Querying `(x,y)` multiplies `H` by `X_x ⊙ Y_y`; the intended value vector is recovered together with crosstalk from the other 255 bindings.
