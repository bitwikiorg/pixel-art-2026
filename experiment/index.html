Experiments

What can a pixel-addressed field represent, preserve, compute, and learn?

The experiments begin with exact binary information, expand to finite color state, and then introduce redundancy, reusable motifs, distributed codes, memory, fixed computation, learned state, architecture comparisons, generative descriptions, and inherited controllers. Each mechanism is measured against the resources it adds.
